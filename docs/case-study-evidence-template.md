## 9. Publication gate

- [ ] Written permission or approved anonymization is recorded.
- [ ] Client, dates, market, scope, and platform details are accurate.
- [ ] Every metric has a definition, source, date range, and calculation.
- [ ] Baseline and comparison periods are genuinely comparable.
- [ ] Personal, payment, account, and customer data are removed.
- [ ] No platform affiliation, badge, office, certification, or guarantee is implied.
- [ ] The client approved its name, logo, quotation, and result wording where used.
- [ ] A second reviewer checked all numbers against the retained evidence.
- [ ] Directory-specific fields match the website and legal company identity.

If any required item is incomplete, keep the case study in draft and do not submit it to a directory.
