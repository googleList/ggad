Policy basis

Google's third-party policy requires providers to be honest and transparent about their company, services, associated costs, and expected results. It also requires a separate account for each end advertiser and prohibits false verification information or helping clients circumvent enforcement. Review the current Google third-party policy and the related account ownership guide.
