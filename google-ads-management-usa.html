Service Scope

A practical system from measurement to optimization

The right scope depends on your offer, margins, sales cycle, location coverage, and existing data. We begin with what can be measured and clearly document responsibilities before campaign work starts.

Account and market review

Review business details, billing readiness, policy-sensitive claims, geographic coverage, prior campaign data, and landing-page alignment before launch.

Search campaign structure

Organize campaigns by service, commercial intent, geography, and landing page. Build keyword themes, negative-keyword controls, and ads that match the actual offer.

Conversion measurement

Define meaningful actions such as qualified forms, calls, booked consultations, purchases, or imported offline outcomes. Validate tags before using automated bidding.

Ongoing management

Review search terms, budget pacing, locations, devices, audiences, creative assets, and conversion quality. Changes are recorded and explained in regular reports.

Pricing Approach

Separate media spend from management work

Google charges the advertising spend directly in your account. Consulting or management fees cover planning, setup, measurement, monitoring, and optimization. A proposal should state both amounts separately.

  • Scope based on campaign count, markets, tracking complexity, and reporting needs.
  • No guaranteed rankings, approval outcomes, lead volume, or return on ad spend.
  • No long-term commitment is implied until deliverables, timing, and fees are confirmed in writing.
  • Your business remains responsible for truthful claims, products, fulfillment, and platform compliance.

Questions

Before starting a US campaign

Do you provide or rent Google Ads accounts?

No. We do not sell, rent, transfer, or hold advertising accounts. Work is performed in an account owned and controlled by the client.

Can you guarantee account approval or a target ROAS?

No. Platform reviews and auction results are controlled by Google. We can identify risks, improve measurement, and test campaign decisions, but we do not promise an approval or financial outcome.

What do you need for an initial review?

Your website, offer, target US locations, approximate monthly media budget, primary conversion action, and any available campaign or analytics history.
