## Submission gate

No directory profile should be submitted until all of the following are true:

- The destination page is live, indexable, canonical, and included in the sitemap.
- Company name, location, services, and contact details match the website.
- Every case study has client permission or is fully anonymized without invented facts.
- Any performance figure has a source period, denominator, and evidence file.
- The directory is relevant to professional marketing services and has transparent terms.
- The listing does not require a followed link, reciprocal link, or review manipulation.

## Local profile rule

Do not create US or Hong Kong Google Business Profiles unless the company begins genuine, verifiable in-person operations there. Google states that eligible profiles require face-to-face customer contact and excludes online-only businesses.

## Measurement

Track each live profile by referring domain, referral sessions, qualified enquiries, branded-search impressions, and assisted conversions. A directory should not be renewed or upgraded based on domain authority alone.
